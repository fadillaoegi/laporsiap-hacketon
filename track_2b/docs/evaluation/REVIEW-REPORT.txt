LaporSiap — technical and evidence review, 10 October 2026

PURPOSE AND APERTUS ROLE
Preparing a report from scattered chats can require copying sources, arranging
claims and listing uncertain transfers. LaporSiap supplies a bilingual local
workspace for this preparation. It does not send an official report, verify a
bank transaction or guarantee recovery. IASC remains linked throughout the app.
Apertus 1.5 70B drafts short statements from source messages. Transfer facts
come exclusively from user input. A complete manual workflow remains available.
Whether AI saves people time has not been established by independent testing.

IMPLEMENTATION
Go 1.26.9/pgx/PostgreSQL 18 and React/TypeScript/Vite. The provider is requested
with model alias swiss-ai/apertus-v1.5-70b, temperature zero and at most 2400
output tokens. Each event must retain its ordered ID and entire exact source
quote. Strict schema rejects extra fields, invented source IDs, altered quotes,
wrong model identity and incomplete responses. Request timeout is 40 seconds,
with no automatic retry or alternate-model fallback. These checks do not prove
that a contract-valid summary has the same meaning as its source.

SOURCE PRESERVATION AND REVIEW
Only known amounts of reported successful transfers are totaled; pending/failed
amounts are excluded. Unknown stays unknown; partially known totals are labelled.
Chat timestamps do not fill transfer dates. Edits invalidate previous review.
Users inspect each source, transfers and limitations before backend permits
PDF/TXT/ZIP export. PNG/JPEG are decoded within limits; PDF validation remains
basic, without OCR, malware scanning or verification of authenticity.

ACCESS, PRIVACY AND COST CONTROLS
Case bearer tokens are random, hashed in the database and kept in sessionStorage.
Cases expire after 30 days, access after seven days. Rotation revokes old access;
confirmed case deletion cascades to history/files. Cleanup runs at startup/hourly.
Backup/download deletion is separate. There is no account or lost-token recovery.
Default masking replaces detected sender names, emails, Indonesian phone numbers,
labelled identifiers/account numbers and user-added terms. Users inspect the exact
payload and consent to its hash/revision/language. Only source IDs/text are sent;
quotes are restored locally after contract validation. Detection is limited,
and original local sources/exports may still contain personal information.
Provider disclosure follows the configured endpoint, including local inference.

Persistent AI attempts: three per case, 20 globally per rolling 24 hours; two active
leases. Failed attempts count, and deletion cannot reset the global meter. Capacity
defaults are 200 cases, 200 revisions and 256 MiB logical case/history/file data.
This is not a physical database/WAL/backup disk limit. API request limiting is
local in-memory, 240 per actual client IP/minute. These limits are not proof of
public-service capacity, distributed abuse prevention or independent security.

NEW DEVELOPMENT EVALUATION
Original frozen dataset bilingual-safety-v1 contains 12 cases: send/refund/promise,
failed payment/no retry, partial totals/unknowns, instructions embedded in sources,
conflicting statuses/correction and masked multiline personal data, in English
and Indonesian. Input hash and all attempts are preserved. There were 10 accepted
drafts, one rejected model identity, and one timeout/connectivity failure. The
returned alternate model identity was not persisted by this evaluation runner;
no claim is made about which alternate model the provider returned.
Assistant review of 26 accepted events found four issues in three Indonesian
drafts: sent-payment assertion omitted with a refund amount association, recruiter
promise attributed to the message sender, failed-payment amount omitted, and the
context of a previous claim omitted. Contract success is not semantic accuracy.
Suggested corrections are documented separately; original outputs were not edited.
Prompt v4 and limited review heuristics address these observed risks. Two new
v4 regression cases timed out. Improved live quality has therefore not been shown.
Read-only database verification found original chat/messages/transfers unchanged
in all 14 cases. Every failed generation retained revision 1 with no draft/review.
There were no retries, fallback or fabricated human results.
